Typography Rules
Do
  • Use Inter (primary font)
  • Lowercase headers in slide decks
  • Azure text for emphasis (not bold)
  • Regular capitalization in body copy
  • Acronyms in ALL CAPS
Don't
  • ALL CAPS (except acronyms)
  • Overuse lowercase (misses context)
  • Title Case headers in running body: "our Process"
Brand Voice
friendly honest transparent trustworthy collaborative genuine knowledgeable cheeky

Simple

Break down complex topics and present them in an accessible way. We are experts — we don't need to prove it with jargon.

Guidance & Education

Help clients understand the decisions and actions needed to make progress. Educate the broader hardware community through blogs, videos, and talks.

Goal Oriented

Always keep a pulse on what is most important to our clients and their goals. Connect our recommendations to their outcomes.

Actionable

Through our phased process, help clients take actionable steps toward measurable outcomes and progress.

Casual Sidebars

Keep things as light and collaborative as possible. We want to get to know our clients as people. Ideally they are someone we can sit down and have a beer with at the end of the day.

Copy Guidelines

Separators

  • | Use pipes in subtitles and signatures — 2 spaces each side
  • | Email signatures: 3 spaces each side of pipe
  • — Hyphens as fallback when pipes are impractical
  • / Slashes as last resort only

Pronouns & Tone

  • → Use "we" not "I" — reinforces studio team, not freelancers
  • → Avoid ALL CAPS — exception: acronyms only
  • → Lowercase headers in slide decks
  • → Regular capitalization in body (not "our Studio, our Process")

Dates

  • → File names: YYMMDD (e.g., 250516)
  • → Running text: no comma between date and year

Tagline Rules

  • ✓ Never in quotes
  • ✓ Period when standalone: what's next.
  • ✓ No period when mid-sentence
  • ✗ Not: "what's next" (no quotes)
Pitch Copy
Short Form
Spanner is a premier Silicon Valley product development studio specializing in the design and engineering of physical products for the world's leading brands — from startups to Fortune 500s — across a range of sectors including consumer electronics, medical tech, industrial, robotics, and more. Spanner's in-house team of experts, composed of mechanical engineers, electrical engineers and industrial designers, provide the full range of product management and development services. From concept generation through prototype and production start, Spanner aligns rigorous product development processes with our clients' business objectives to create delightful products and business successes, together.
Long Form
Spanner specializes in the design and engineering of physical products for the world's leading brands — from startups to Fortune 500s — across a range of sectors including Consumer Electronics, Medical Tech, AgTech, Industrial, Robotics, Automotive, and more.

We align rigorous product development processes with our clients' business objectives to create delightful products and business successes, together.

Spanner was created to deliver a superior approach for engaging with innovators, entrepreneurs and designers. This is achieved through a proven, highly collaborative process, in which Spanner teams up with its clients through all aspects of product evolution — from idea to launch. We are dedicated to making the world a better place through our products and beyond.
Stats to include: 150+ clients served  |  500+ projects completed  |  400+ years cumulative expertise  |  20+ years average experience
